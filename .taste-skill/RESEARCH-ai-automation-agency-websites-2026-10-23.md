# Research: What high-converting AI automation agency websites have (2026-10-23)

## Sources
- Goodish.agency/ai-automation — real boutique automation agency (best reference: honest, specific, human-in-the-loop)
- VeloxThemes "How to Design an AAA Website That Converts" + Delani "AI Agency Website Examples" + Collabas "Essential Pages"
- GitHub: Maverick-Limitless/AgencyLaunch-Kit (purpose-built AI-automation-agency starter, MIT)
- GitHub: chiragksharma/BuildFast (61★ SaaS landing boilerplate — section library)

## The universal section pattern (all sources agree)
1. Hero: outcome + who + result, ONE CTA ("book a free audit"). Never "powering the future with AI".
2. Social proof immediately after hero: stat chips (Goodish: Free audit / 24-7 / 2 own AI products / partner logos)
3. "What can we automate FIRST": 4-7 named use-cases, each with a 1-line outcome + tool chain (e.g. Lead qualification — Typeform·OpenAI·Zoho)
4. Plain-language definition of the service (Goodish's is the model)
5. Trust differentiator section (Goodish: "why every workflow has a human approval step")
6. "What we built ourselves" — own products/systems as proof (HMS advantage: TrendLoop, Data Refinery, trading bot, blog pipeline)
7. How we build: 4-step technical process (Trigger → AI → Review → Action)
8. How an engagement runs: 3-phase (Audit & strategy → Build → Launch & handover)
9. Interactive savings calculator (AgencyLaunch-Kit's standout feature — estimate $ saved per workflow)
10. Services: 3-5 outcome-named, grouped (Build/Integrate/Scale), NOT tech jargon
11. Case studies: before/after + ≥1 number + honest timeline. New agencies: label illustrative examples clearly, never invent proof.
12. Pricing: starting-from + 3 tiers (Audit/Build/Scale); transparent beats gatekeeping
13. FAQ: 5-6 real questions (cost, models used, will AI touch my CRM alone, timeline, how to start)
14. Testimonials: specific and numbered ("saved 40+ hrs/mo"), not "great service"
15. Tool/partner logo strip (real systems: Shopify, eBay, n8n, OpenAI...)
16. Related articles funnel

## Gap list for hms-site v3 (current homepage)
MISSING: process, named use-cases w/ tool chains, calculator, FAQ, pricing approach, immediate
social-proof strip, before/after case studies, testimonials, tool logos, blog-as-proof funnel.
HAVE ALREADY (strengths to keep): live-ops dashboard panel (real numbers = Goodish's "own products
in production" energy), work blocks, real product photography, operator positioning, 12-post blog.

proof beats big fake proof. HMS real numbers we can use: 17 SKUs live, 10,255 properties enriched,
12 posts automated, 48h turnaround, first sale shipped+tracked 10/19, 20+ automations running.
