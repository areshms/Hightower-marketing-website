## Universal section pattern (synthesised from all sources)
1. **Hero** — outcome + who + result, ONE CTA, real imagery
2. **Stat strip / social proof** — immediately after hero, real numbers only
3. **Who we are / story** — honest, operator-led, with real pictures
4. **Services / capabilities** — named use-cases with tool chains, outcome-focused
5. **Work / portfolio** — named projects with results, before/after framing
6. **Process** — 3-5 steps, how an engagement runs
7. **Pricing approach** — transparent, fixed-quote-after-audit
8. **FAQ** — 5-6 real questions, native details
9. **CTA / contact** — clear next step
10. **Footer** — designed close

## Design direction: sentence-as-layout (seed 167534680)
- **Hero**: "Hightower Marketing" on one line with a real product photo replacing a letter/inline
- **Type**: Playfair Display + Inter (kept from brand)
- **Palette**: gold-deep/paper/ink/coal (kept from brand)
- **Idea**: "On this page, **our real storefront products** become **the letters in the headline**"
- **Sections**: about (Portrait as drop cap) → work (Names as paragraph) → features (Sentences with switches) → proof → stats → how-it-works → pricing → faq → cta
