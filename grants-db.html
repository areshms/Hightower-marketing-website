FAQ

Everything you need to know before subscribing.

How is this different from free grants.gov search?

Grants.gov mixes thousands of opportunities across all applicant types. This sheet filters to small-business eligibility, keeps it live weekly, and explains eligibility and cost-sharing in plain English — plus the playbook tab that tells you what to have ready before you apply.

Where does the data come from?

Directly from the official Grants.gov API. This product uses the Grants.gov API but is not endorsed or certified by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. Each row links to the official listing — the authoritative source — so you always read the NOFO before applying.

Does the subscription guarantee I win a grant?

No. This is a data and readiness product: it tells you which opportunities are open to small businesses and how to become application-ready. It does not guarantee awards and is not legal, financial, or tax advice.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month through Stripe. Cancel any time and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Grant-specific award ranges, effort scoring, state and foundation coverage, deadline-alert emails, and an API tier for agents and workflow tools.
