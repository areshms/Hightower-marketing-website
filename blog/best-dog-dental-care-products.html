FAQ

Do dental chews actually work?

They help with plaque at the gum line and most dogs love them — that compliance is their superpower. They supplement brushing; nothing replaces the mechanical action of a toothbrush.

How often should I brush my dog's teeth?

Daily is the gold standard, 3x/week is realistic for most households, and something always beats nothing. Pair it with something the dog loves so it becomes routine, not battle.

When does my dog actually need a dental cleaning under anesthesia?

If you see brown tartar building at the gum line, red gums, or bad breath that brushing doesn't fix, that's a vet conversation. Home care prevents; it doesn't reverse buildup.
