Two budgets get confused here, so let's separate them. Ad spend goes straight to TikTok — you set it and they bill it. Management covers the work: creative, campaign builds, and reporting. What moves the work side up or down:

  • Creative volume. TikTok audiences fatigue faster than search audiences; a healthy account needs fresh hooks, cuts, and variations feeding it continuously.
  • Where clicks land. TikTok Shop, a Shopify store, and a landing page each need different event tracking and post-click setup.
  • Product breadth. One hero product is a test plan; twenty SKUs are a testing calendar.

As with everything we do, the proposal is fixed-scope before work starts: exact deliverables per week, what's produced in-house, and how reporting works.
