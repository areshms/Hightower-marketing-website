How to choose the right pet

Three things matter most when comparing pet:

  • Budget: in this category, the $15–$50 range hits the quality sweet spot — below that you trade away durability, above it you're often paying for brand name.
  • Use case: match the product to where it'll live — a kids' room needs softer light and tough materials, a living space can go decorative.
  • Power source: USB-chargeable picks (most of this list) are cheaper to run and safer around kids than plug-in-only options.
