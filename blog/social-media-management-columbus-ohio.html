What Social Media Management costs in Columbus (2026)

Columbus pricing runs close to the national median for marketing services. Most local providers quote per project or monthly retainer; always ask what's included in reporting. Our Social Media Management service page has our exact packages.

Management quotes swing widely because "social media" can mean four different jobs. When you compare providers, price these separately in your head:

  • Platforms. Each one is its own format and rhythm. Three platforms done properly beats five done badly.
  • Cadence. The posting calendar drives the workload — three posts a week costs less than daily.
  • Video versus static. Short video with voiceover, captions, and cuts takes far more production time than a photo and caption.
  • Community management. Publishing is one job; answering comments and DMs is another. Know which one you're buying.

Our proposals are fixed-scope: exact platforms, exact post counts, and what reporting you get, agreed before work starts.
