What SEO costs in Columbus (2026)

Columbus pricing runs close to the national median for marketing services. Most local providers quote per project or monthly retainer; always ask what's included in reporting. Our SEO service page has our exact packages.

SEO pricing varies more than most services because the work itself varies. A ten-page site that has never had a technical review is a different job than a 300-URL site with duplicate pages and dead redirects. What actually moves the price:

  • Site size and history. Every page gets crawled, assessed, and either fixed or pruned — more pages and more history means more work.
  • How competitive your market is. Ranking a Reynoldsburg HVAC page against national lead-gen sites is a different workload than a niche B2B service with few local rivals.
  • Content depth. Pages that mainly need titles, headings, and internal links cost less to fix than pages that need writing from scratch.
  • The technical plumbing. If Search Console, sitemaps, canonicals, and schema were never set up, that work comes first — and it's usually the highest-certainty win available.

Proposals are fixed-scope: you see the audit findings, the prioritized fix list, and what each phase delivers before anything is signed.

What's included in our SEO work

Every engagement starts with a technical audit: we crawl each page, check indexation in Search Console, and list what's broken — canonical conflicts, missing sitemap entries, orphan pages, schema gaps. Then the on-page layer: titles and meta descriptions that match real search phrasing, heading structure, and internal links between pages that help each other. Local signals get tightened too — consistent name, address, and phone everywhere on and off the site, plus a Google Business Profile review. You get weekly plain-English reporting on what actually moved.
