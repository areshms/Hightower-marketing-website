What Web Design costs in Columbus (2026)

Columbus pricing runs close to the national median for marketing services. Most local providers quote per project or monthly retainer; always ask what's included in reporting. Our Web Design service page has our exact packages.

We won't pretend one number fits every project — honest pricing depends on what your site needs to do. What we can tell you is exactly what moves a quote up or down:

  • Page count and complexity. A five-page brochure site is a different job than one with location pages, a booking flow, and a blog.
  • Storefront or not. A Shopify checkout means product data, payments, shipping rules, and test orders — all wired and verified before launch.
  • Who writes the words. Solid copy and real photos speed the build; writing or editing content is project time too.
  • Integrations. Email platforms, CRMs, booking software, and inventory feeds each add setup and testing.
  • Life after launch. Updates, monitoring, and reporting are a separate line from the build — ask any provider how they handle it.

Every proposal we send is fixed-scope before work starts: deliverables listed, revision rounds agreed, and what late changes cost. If someone quotes you a magic number without asking what your site must do, that's usually a template in disguise.

What's included in every build we ship

Each site leaves our shop with the foundations done: a mobile-first layout checked on real phones, copy edited for clarity, a clean heading structure, canonical tags and a sitemap, schema markup where it applies, Google Search Console verified with analytics wired in, and contact forms that land in an inbox you actually check. Hosting, SSL, and deployment to your domain are handled by us.

What we need from you

You supply what only you can: your logo and brand colors if they exist, the business facts — services, hours, service area, contact details — real photos, access to your domain or DNS, and the five questions customers ask you most. That last list usually becomes your FAQ page, and it's better copy than anything we could invent.

How our process works

We keep the steps boring on purpose. Boring is what gets projects delivered on time:

  1. Free consult — we look at where you stand today: current site, Google presence, and what customers actually find (book here). You get a straight read even if the answer is "keep what you have."
  2. Proposal in 48 hours — fixed scope, fixed price, with the page list and deliverables spelled out.
  3. Discovery and content — we collect the facts, photos, and copy input above, then agree the page outline before design starts.
  4. Design draft on staging — you review a live preview link, not PDFs. Two revision rounds are standard.
  5. Launch — deploy to your domain, verify Search Console, submit the sitemap, and test every form and link one last time.
  6. Weekly reporting — plain-English numbers on traffic and inquiries, no vanity metrics.

Redesigning an existing site? Nothing disappears: we map your current URLs first, keep the ones earning their keep, and 301-redirect everything that changes so old links and search listings land in the right place.
