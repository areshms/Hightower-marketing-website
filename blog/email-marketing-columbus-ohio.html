What Email Marketing costs in Columbus (2026)

Columbus pricing runs close to the national median for marketing services. Most local providers quote per project or monthly retainer; always ask what's included in reporting. Our Email Marketing service page has our exact packages.

Email has two costs, and it pays to keep them separate: your platform bills its own subscription based on list size, and the work is everything else — flows, campaigns, cleanup, and reporting. What moves the work side up or down:

  • Flows versus one-off campaigns. Automated flows — welcome, abandoned cart, win-back — are built once and run every day. More setup now, far less babysitting later.
  • The state of your list. An old, unsorted list needs hygiene before anything sends: invalid addresses, duplicates, and contacts that stopped opening.
  • Integrations. Tying email to your store or CRM so orders and signups flow in automatically, instead of someone pasting CSV files by hand.

Proposals are fixed-scope: the flows listed, the campaign cadence agreed, and reporting defined before work starts.

What's included in the service

We start with a deliverability audit — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC aligned on your sending domain, because the best email in the world is worthless sitting in a spam folder. Then list hygiene: invalid and duplicate contacts pruned, dead addresses sunset, and a policy set going forward. From there the flows: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, and win-back, each written in plain copy you approve before it ever sends. Campaigns follow on the cadence we agree, on templates that match your brand. Reporting focuses on what's real — clicks, replies, and orders — because open rates have become unreliable numbers most people ignore.

What we need from you

Your list export with how each signup got there, your platform login (Klaviyo, Mailchimp, your CRM — whichever you run), your offer and promo calendar, product facts we can write from, and an honest sense of how often you're willing to email people. That last one shapes everything else.

How often should I email my list?

Often enough to stay familiar, rarely enough to stay wanted. For most small businesses that's a meaningful email every week or few, not a daily grind. We'd rather tell you that than sell a volume you'll regret.

My list is old and dormant. Can it be saved?
