FAQ

How often should I deshed my dog?

Heavy shedders: 2-3 times a week during shedding season, weekly otherwise. Light coats: weekly brushing is plenty and doubles as a coat-health check.

Do deshedding tools work on short-haired dogs?

Yes — undercoat rakes and rubber curry-style tools pull dead undercoat from short coats too. The FURminator-class tools shine most on double coats; short-haired dogs do fine with simpler slickers.

Why does my dog shed more in spring?

Photoperiod triggers coat blow — longer daylight = winter coat release. It's normal, it's temporary, and it's exactly when the right tool saves your furniture.
