What cities does the data cover?

Columbus, Cleveland, Dayton, Akron, Canton, Toledo, Youngstown, and Cincinnati — 8 counties sourced from live county auditor ArcGIS records. That's 88,688 plex properties (2-50 units) with owner and mailing address data.

Will I get the actual owner names and addresses?

Yes — the starter lead list includes property address, property type, unit count, and a lead score. Owner names are available through the full $300/mo sheet or via our skip-trace endpoint ($0.25/record). We do not display owner names on public pages or in the workshop slides.

All data comes from public county auditor records — property transfers, tax records, GIS parcels, and zoning. It's the same data you could access yourself through county websites; we just clean, score, and deliver it at scale. Always verify independently and comply with local laws before contacting owners.
